Bio

I am Jiwon Choi, an M.S. student in AI Computing at KAIST and a member of the KAIST Visual AI Group, advised by Prof. Minhyuk Sung. I received my B.S. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Yonsei University.

My research interests are in generative modeling and reinforcement learning. Recently, I have been exploring online/offline reinforcement learning of generative-model-based policies for continuous action planning.

For details, refer to my Curriculum Vitae (CV).
